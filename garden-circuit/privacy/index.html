Garden Circuit: Privacy Policy

Version 3. Effective date: 15 October 2026

Who we are

Garden Circuit is a puzzle game for Android made by Pointy Games, an individual developer. In this policy, "we" means Pointy Games. You can reach us at gamespointy@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

Garden Circuit works offline. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and it has no in-app purchases. Your progress and settings are saved in a file on your device. The game does not send this file to anyone. We do not run our own server for the game. If you uninstall the game, the saved file is deleted with it.

How the game adapts to you (on your device only)

To keep puzzles fair and fun, the game keeps simple statistics about how you play in the same saved file: for example how many levels you won or failed, how often you used hints, undo or restart, how long your sessions are, how many days you have played, whether you watched or skipped the optional ads, and the country from your phone's language and region setting. The game uses them, on your device, to:

  • give a little extra help when you are stuck, such as a free hint or an extra turn, or a few more turns on a retry;
  • occasionally give one turn fewer to confident players (every level always stays solvable with the turns shown);
  • decide how often ads appear between levels (fewer for new players and players who are having a hard time).

These statistics are kept on your device and are deleted when you uninstall the game. Separately, the game reports anonymous usage events and crash reports through Google Firebase (see below).

Ads and Google

The game shows two kinds of ads:

  • Optional rewarded ads. You can choose to watch a short video ad to get 3 extra turns after you fail a level, to get an extra hint, or to keep your daily streak after missing a day. Nothing happens unless you choose to watch.
  • Ads between levels. After you win a level, a full-screen ad can sometimes appear before the next screen. These never appear in your first two sessions, in the first levels, after a failed level, or during a tutorial, and there is a limit on how often they show.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the game. Google's User Messaging Platform is used to ask for your consent where it is required.

When the game shows or loads ads, Google's SDK automatically collects and uses this information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID.
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location.
  • How you interact with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic information about how the app and the SDK perform, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use.

Google receives this information directly from your device. Pointy Games only sees summary reports from Google, such as how many ads were shown, which do not identify you. To show ads, the game needs internet access, and it uses the related Android permissions (internet, network state and the advertising ID). The game also uses the vibrate permission for its haptics setting.

How Google uses this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

To find bugs and see which levels are too hard or too easy, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google. They collect:

  • Usage events, such as starting, winning or failing a level, using a hint, and whether an optional ad was watched.
  • Crash reports: the state of the app when it crashed, the device model, the Android version and similar technical details.
  • A Firebase installation ID and app instance ID (random identifiers for this installation of the game), the advertising ID where allowed, and your IP address (used to estimate the country).

Pointy Games sees this data in Firebase, in aggregate. It does not include your name, email or contacts. Google processes it under its own terms: Firebase privacy and security. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months; crash reports for up to 90 days. Where the consent form applies, analytics uses Google's consent mode and follows your choice.

Consent and choices

  • Consent form. Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows a consent form when you first open it. No ads are requested until you have made your choice.
  • Privacy options. Where the consent form applies, a "Privacy options" button appears in Settings and in the Pause menu. Use it to review or change your choice at any time.
  • Android ad settings. On your device you can open Settings, then Google, then Ads, to delete your advertising ID or reset it, and to opt out of ad personalisation.
  • Skip the ads. Rewarded ads are optional, and you can play the whole game without watching any of them. Ads between levels can be closed once their close button appears.

Children

Garden Circuit is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the game and you have a concern, please contact us.

Data retention and deletion

Saved progress and settings stay on your device until you uninstall the game, which deletes them.

How to ask for your data to be deleted: email gamespointy@gmail.com with the subject "Garden Circuit data deletion". Because the game has no accounts, include the "Player ID" shown at the bottom of the game's Settings screen, so we can find the Firebase records for your installation. We delete the analytics and crash data for that ID within 30 days and confirm by email. Data that Google holds for ads is managed by Google; you can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings. Information that Google collects for ads is kept under Google's own retention practices, described in the Google links above. If you have a question about your data, write to gamespointy@gmail.com.

Security

Your saved data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Google states that the data its SDK collects is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example by adding in-app purchases, we will update this policy and change the effective date at the top of this page. The current version is always on this page.
